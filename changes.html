Deliberately excluded: unverified history & rumors

Roster-move histories, “drama”, and substitute-player claims circulate widely — including on the design-reference hub that inspired this site's layout. We treat all of it as Unverified until each move is re-checked against its own announcement. Repeating unsourced claims would launder them into apparent facts, so this page stays short on purpose.
