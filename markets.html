How a price enters this project

  1. Find: query bounded public endpoint scopes (Polymarket search + CS tag; Kalshi CS2 match-winner and outright series). Coverage is not exhaustive.
  2. Capture: record the first-party best ask (not midpoint), top-of-book size, source timestamp, query URL, market ID, selection, settlement rule and capture timestamp. Reopening a live URL later may show a different book; stored exact fields are the historical receipt.
  3. Preserve: publish append-only snapshots. A failed check is an explicit alert, not a zero-price observation. No bets from offline research replay or resolved 0/1 markets.
  4. Settle: not automated yet. Venue resolution and independently confirmed event result must agree, including unusual void or 50/50 rules; pending remains pending.

Why no sportsbook odds yet?

No terms-compliant, documented free sportsbook odds endpoint has been verified for this project. That is a coverage gap, not proof no free source exists. We will not bypass paid APIs, login walls, robots policies or source terms. The first implementation uses public exchange APIs; traditional sportsbook lines can only enter with equally auditable and permitted sourcing.

Tournament betting context (verified facts)

  • Short event, big variance: 8 teams, GSL groups (Bo3), single-elim playoffs (Bo3, Bo5 final) — favorites have little room to recover from one bad series (ML-011, ML-012).
  • A 3rd-place decider is played (Bo3), and 3rd pays double 4th ($100k vs $50k) — placement markets must account for it (ML-012, ML-006).
  • Top-heavy field: Falcons and FURIA were the only top-five sides in the cited HLTV Sep 16 article; Legacy and Aurora were also top-ten at that snapshot (ML-009).
  • Qualifier seed: Virtus.pro (organizer reveal VRS #30 on Sep 16, not a live rank) came through online qualifying into a LAN final — the classic longshot profile (ML-019).
