How the Finals work SOURCE-BASED

  1. Groups: two four-team double-elimination (GSL) groups; every series is best of three. Each group starts with two opening series, then a winners' match, an elimination match and a decider; the top two advance. An opening loss does not automatically eliminate a team. Liquipedia + HLTV ↗
  2. Playoffs: single elimination; all series best of three except the best-of-five Grand Final. HLTV lists a best-of-three third-place decider. The winner of the semifinal is not guaranteed first place. Format sources ↗
  3. Prize money: $1m total, half player share / half club share. The 3rd-place total is $100k vs $50k for 4th — important if a placement contract distinguishes them. Full prize table ↗
  4. Map pool published for the event: Ancient, Anubis, Cache, Dust II, Inferno, Mirage and Nuke. This is a seven-map list, not a claim that a particular match veto or map order is known. Liquipedia map list ↗

Fixture integrity: a prediction venue may list two teams before the organizer publishes the draw. Our parser surfaces such a matchup as an unconfirmed signal until it is checked against independent event/match sources; it cannot drive a paper position.

For a $1-settling share with best ask p, a gross illustrative payout factor is 1/p. At an example hypothetical ask of $0.62, 1/0.62 ≈ 1.61. If two sides imply more than 100% in total, the difference may reflect spreads/fees rather than a guaranteed opportunity. Real execution and venue fees are not modeled here.

What we still cannot answer

No verified Finals draw, no complete reserve lists, no per-player performance-window stats and no full team-drama history are in this dataset. We do not manufacture a five-map veto, fair probability, recent K/D or a traded price to fill an empty card. Check the limitations/next-work plan and claim-by-claim audit list.
