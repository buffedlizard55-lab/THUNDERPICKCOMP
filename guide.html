How the Finals work SOURCE-BASED

  1. Groups: two four-team double-elimination (GSL) groups; every series is best of three. Each group starts with two opening series, then a winners' match, an elimination match and a decider; the top two advance. An opening loss does not automatically eliminate a team. Liquipedia + HLTV ↗
  2. Playoffs: single elimination; all series best of three except the best-of-five Grand Final. HLTV lists a best-of-three third-place decider. The winner of the semifinal is not guaranteed first place. Format sources ↗
  3. Prize money: $1m total, half player share / half club share. The 3rd-place total is $100k vs $50k for 4th — important if a placement contract distinguishes them. Full prize table ↗
  4. Map pool published for the event: Ancient, Anubis, Cache, Dust II, Inferno, Mirage and Nuke. This is a seven-map list, not a claim that a particular match veto or map order is known. Liquipedia map list ↗ — and Cache replaced Overpass Jul 8, 2026 (Anubis replaced Train Jan 22).
  5. Core-roster & stand-in rules: each team must keep 3/5 of its Aug 3 VRS snapshot five or face technical loss; max 8 registered, one stand-in allowed (72h registration, 15-min approval), emergency stand-in may be confidential. §3.5 · §4.1/4.4 server & veto.
  6. Veto & servers: Bo3 veto A-ban B-ban A-pick B-pick A-ban B-ban → decider (knife for side); MR12 $800/1:55/20s freeze/40s bomb/3×30s timeouts; OT MR3 $12,500 +1 timeout. Rulebook ↗

Fixture integrity: a prediction venue may list two teams before the organizer publishes the draw. Our parser surfaces such a matchup as an unconfirmed signal until it is checked against independent event/match sources; it cannot drive a paper position. Also 7 of 8 TWC teams play EPL S24 Oct 3-11 right before Malta — fatigue/strat-hiding flag.

Betting strategies for this format — for market-experienced readers ANALYSIS · NOT ADVICE

These are testable strategy theses (mirrored on the simulated leaderboard), not guaranteed edges. Every thesis is a hypothesis to check against sourced data and your own model — never a recommendation to place a real wager.

1 · Favorite vs value

Short GSL→single-elim with only two top-five sides can inflate favorite outrights. sim_favorite_backer backs the better Sep-16 VRS side flat 25u; sim_vrs_value needs an 8-point gap between market-implied and a VRS-gap heuristic (2.5% per rank). Check depth, timestamp, and core-roster compliance before inferring value.

2 · Underdog & variance

sim_underdog_hunter / sim_cache_chaos / sim_contrarian_cap exploit volatility: fresh Cache pool (~3mo data), opening Bo3s where veto prep matters, and dogs at ≥3.00 decimal. Validate vs recent form and map veto history — don't blindly fade.

3 · Qualifier fade & fatigue

sim_qualifier_fade fades VP in group matches (VRS #30→#40). Counter: VP skips EPL S24 while 7 others face fatigue/strat-hiding; 4v5 continuity (10 rounds or +6) and confidential emergency stand-ins can flip a map. Check withdrawal rules (§2.4.5).

4 · Outright correlation (only live paper policy)

sim_champ_correlation atomically logs 50u on FURIA+Falcons only if both have fresh first-party best asks with size ≥ stake/ask in the same pre-Oct 14 live run. No fees modeled; payout gross stake/p. Past qualifier 0/1 resolutions are never backfilled. See Ledger & settlement.

Risk discipline: bankroll-share sizing, checking prize-cluster jumps ($100k vs $50k for 3rd/4th), and reading each market's void/50-50 text beat chasing a "sure thing." This site labels simulated P/L SIM; never imply a paper decision was a real fill.
