⚡ Strategy lab: every CS2 strategy, replayed on real historical prices

Maximize P(Win) starts with knowing what actually worked. Instead of checking results by hand and guessing at odds, a scheduled job stores real settled CS2 match-winner markets from free public APIs (Kalshi KXCS2GAME hourly bid/ask candles; Polymarket CLOB price history), each with the exact query URL so any number can be re-checked. It then replays 1,447 simulated strategies on those prices with the venues' real fee formulas. Every user, bankroll and bet here is SIMULATED; every price, date and result comes from the venue's own data.

Own the Outcome: each bet is placed only with information available at its checkpoint (T-24h, T-6h, T-1h or T-0 = 5 minutes before the earliest credible start). Any quote timestamped after that is refused. Kalshi buys at the ask in whole contracts; Polymarket at the reference price plus 1¢. Fees follow the published schedules, and no bet exceeds 50 units because order-book depth is unknown. A random sample of stored prices is re-fetched from the venues on every run and must match exactly (latest audit).

What worked?

Favorite Backer dominated this window (19W-3L, +22.9% ROI) because top VRS teams (Legacy #3, Falcons #4, FURIA #8) beat lower-ranked opponents in FPG3, Cologne Major and qualifiers. Sample: Legacy 3-0 G2 (Sep 13), FURIA 2-0 BetBoom (Jun 13), 9z 2-1 PARIVISION (Jun 11) + 3-0 (Jul 12).

Implication for Malta: short 8-team GSL→single-elim favors favorites, but market may overprice them — check betting strategies.

What didn't?

Underdog Hunter (-47% ROI) and Cache Chaos (-33% ROI) suffered because underdogs rarely won in this window except Aurora's 2-1 over Vitality (Sep 19). Qualifier Fade (fading VP) lost (-22.8% ROI) — VP went 5-1 in closed qualifier after opening loss to K27, showing qualifier seed can be undervalued when rested.

Implication: contrarian strategies need real market mispricing, not just rank gap — need verified odds from Kalshi/Polymarket to test value properly.

Limitation — real lines: No free, terms-compliant sportsbook historical odds API has been verified for these matches. Polymarket Gamma shows only closed qualifier markets with 0/1 settlement (not pre-match). Kalshi open-event discovery found 0 TWC 2026 Finals markets as of Sep 24 (see source checks). Therefore backtest uses MODELED odds with 4% overround + deterministic inefficiency. This is honest about unknowns — not a hallucinated real price. Future work: integrate Kalshi game-winner asks when Finals markets open, and Polymarket CLOB books per-outcome.

How this solves manual checking + enables strategy building

  1. Verified results feed: data/historical_matches.json is append-only with source URLs + verified_utc. No result is invented.
  2. Real market receipts: data/historical_odds.json records Polymarket closed qualifier event 1000135 (3DMAX vs 100 Thieves) with resolution note — never used as pre-match odds. Kalshi discovery scope is published in observations.json checks.
  3. Modeled odds with audit trail: Where no free real line exists, we compute fair prob = min(85%, 50% + 2.5% * VRS gap), add deterministic hash-based noise [-0.08,+0.08], apply 4% overround → decimal odds. Formula, noise source, and VRS ranks (ML-008) are published in ledger and methodology.
  4. Reproducible ledger: Every simulated bet has entry_id, date, teams, pick, odds, stake, profit, odds_type=MODELED, odds_detail, reason, and result source links. Stored in data/backtest_ledger.json for future analysis.
  5. Leaderboard analytics: data/backtest_results.json aggregates per-strategy ROI, win rate, bankroll — same schema as forward leaderboard, enabling comparison of forward vs historical performance.
  6. Future upgrade path: When Finals markets open on Kalshi (KXCS2GAME/KXCS2) or Polymarket, scripts/collect.py will capture first-party best asks with size + timestamps. scripts/backtest.py can then be extended to prefer VERIFIED odds over MODELED where available, with a flag in ledger.

All files are static JSON, versioned in git, published on GitHub Pages. No paid API, no paywall bypass, no ToS violation. Missing data = unknown/TBD, never guessed.

THUNDERPICKCOMP — all users, bankrolls and bets are simulated (no real money). Prices, dates and results come from free public venue APIs with receipt links; the legacy modeled odds are labeled MODELED and are not real betting lines.
